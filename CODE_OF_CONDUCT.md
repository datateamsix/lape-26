# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in LAPE-26 a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, musical training, or research discipline.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, methods, evidence, and code rather than people.
- Distinguish creative interpretation from scientific evidence.
- Credit contributors and data sources accurately.
- Welcome questions from non-specialists.
- Disclose limitations, conflicts of interest, and uncertainty.
- Respect privacy in listener-study data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate misrepresentation of research, plagiarism, or publishing private participant information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer through the security/contact channel listed in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
